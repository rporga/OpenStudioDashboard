Method

Four equal-weight categories

Download validation CSV

Each category score is the sum of normalized assessed criterion values divided by its assessed criterion count. Each overall Nestlé or WPP score is the equal-weight average of its assessed category scores. The combined score is secondary and is calculated only when both overall scores are available.
