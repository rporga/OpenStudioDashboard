TAT

How to read this view

Median TAT shows the typical completed brief

TAT is measured once per unique brief, from Project Brief Date to its latest completed Project Delivery Date. The median is the default because a small number of long-running scopes can distort the average.

  • No duplication: repeated deliverable rows are grouped using market, brand, project title and brief date.
  • Standard scope: completed briefs with valid dates and a duration of 90 calendar days or less.
  • Separate classifications: creation, adaptation, mixed and unclassified briefs are mutually exclusive.
